FAQ

How does the free trial work?

Run the local test server with MOCK_STEAM=true. Full crypto flow works without a credit card. When you're ready for production, choose a plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts. Cancel from your dashboard. Your DLCs remain encrypted — existing offline tokens continue working until they expire.

What happens if the service goes down?

Offline tokens continue working for their full TTL (24-72h). Players who authenticated recently are unaffected.

Do I need to modify my game?

3 lines of C# code. Copy two files into your Unity project. Configure three fields. Done.
